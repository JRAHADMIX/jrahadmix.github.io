Personal assistant · Application information

A clearer view
of the working day.

Jarvis helps its owner review email, calendar events and documents, prepare daily briefings and draft replies.

Jarvis runs in a separate private environment; this website is not the assistant interface.

Email

Find messages, review conversations and prepare reply drafts for the owner to check.

Calendar

Read upcoming events and bring relevant reminders into a daily briefing.

Documents

Search Google Drive and use relevant document information for the owner's requested work.

Google access

The current Google connection requests read-only access to Gmail, Calendar and Drive. These permissions do not allow Jarvis to send mail, modify calendar events or change Drive files through this connection.

How processing works

Jarvis runs on the owner's computer and uses external AI services to produce summaries and drafts. Relevant content may be processed through the Anthropic API. Briefings and notifications may be delivered to the owner's configured Telegram conversation.

Jarvis is for its owner's personal use. This website does not offer public registration, collect Google credentials or display private messages or documents.
