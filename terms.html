Purpose and availability

Jarvis is a personal assistant operated by its owner. It is not currently offered as a public subscription service. This website only describes the application and its data practices.

Authorized use

The owner connects accounts they are authorized to access. The current Google integration has read-only permissions. Drafting an email does not send it. Any separate capability that performs external actions requires its own applicable authorization.

Review of generated output

AI-generated summaries, drafts and suggested actions may contain errors or omit relevant context. The owner should check important statements against their original sources before acting. Reports can be incomplete if sources are unavailable or only part of the history has been retrieved.

Access and interruptions

Operation depends on the owner's computer, connectivity and external services. Availability is not guaranteed. The owner may disable the application, stop its schedules or revoke its Google authorization.
