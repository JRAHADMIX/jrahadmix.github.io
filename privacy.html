Privacy policy

Draft updated 12 October 2026 · Awaiting owner adoption

Draft information for owner review. This policy has not yet been adopted.

Purpose and access

Jarvis is a personal assistant maintained by JRAHADMIX. It uses authorized Google data to review email, prepare drafts and briefings, summarize calendar events and find documents. Its Google connection requests read-only permissions: gmail.readonly, calendar.readonly and drive.readonly.

Processing and delivery

Relevant content is processed through the Anthropic API for assistant features. Reports may be delivered through Telegram. Processing is therefore not exclusively local. This public information website does not display private Google data or provide access to the assistant.

Retention

  • Completed assistant sessions are configured for deletion after 90 days.
  • The latest 50 reports are retained for each scheduled task.
  • Saved conversation snapshots and local drafts remain until manually deleted.

These limits apply to their respective records, not automatically to every copy.

Anthropic

Anthropic states that commercial API inputs and outputs are not used for model training by default, with exceptions for voluntary feedback or explicit permission. Account-specific settings have not been verified. See its training policy and retention policy. No zero-retention guarantee is made.

Stopping access and deleting data

The owner can revoke Google authorization and stop Google-related scheduled features. Previously saved data must be deleted separately. Local copies require local deletion; Telegram copies require deletion in Telegram. Requests about data already sent to Anthropic follow its privacy procedures. Revoking access does not itself delete existing copies.

Limited Use

The proposed policy limits Google data use to the assistant features described above and prohibits selling it, using it for advertising or using it for generalized model training. Use and transfer of Google API data must comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
